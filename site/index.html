OwnChart is not a record viewer. You bring your data — every PDF, FHIR bundle, CCDA, HealthKit metric, faxed report, screenshot, and personal note you can pull together — and OwnChart brings structure, memory, retrieval, citations, and AI-assisted reasoning. You ask questions of your own health life. OwnChart answers with evidence, cites every source, and admits what it doesn't know.

You stay the final authority on what anything means.

The doctrine

Six non-negotiables. Read them as constraints on every feature decision, not aspirations.

The patient owns it

Self-hosted on your hardware. No SaaS backend. No vendor telemetry. No institutional override. The patient is the user, the owner, and the canonical authority on their own story.

Raw sources are immutable

Original PDFs, FHIR bundles, CCDA XML, and uploaded images are stored content-addressed by SHA-256 and never modified. The source is the record. Everything else is a derivation.

User correction is canonical

When the source says one thing and you say another, your version becomes the displayed truth. The source is preserved as evidence, never erased.

One consent gate, on egress

No PHI leaves the host to any LLM provider without your explicit, scoped opt-in. The gate is uniform across providers.

AI cites everything

Every substantive AI statement is source-backed, user-canonical, inferred, statistical, or explicitly unknown. Every claim opens to "why do you think that?"

Significance over fact-count

Source density is not meaning. A dense year of 1,200 extracted facts is not automatically a meaningful year. The product ranks by user-confirmable significance.

What you can bring in

Patient-mediated data, with the originals kept.

Records and FHIR

FHIR R4 from Epic, athenahealth, ModMed, NextGen, and Oracle Health (additional vendors are documented or in progress). CCDA continuity-of-care XML. PDF chart exports. Originals preserved; structured facts extracted.

Capture what APIs miss

Upload screenshots from CVS, portal pages, vaccine records, PDFs, handwritten notes, and photos. OwnChart keeps the original image, extracts structured facts when it can, and cites the source.

Apple Health / HealthKit

Activity, heart, sleep, HRV, workouts, weight, mindfulness, medications, and clinical records — synced from the iOS companion with per-metric privacy controls and idempotent re-syncs.

Voice and personal notes

On-device transcribed voice memos, free-text annotations, and corrections layered on top of any source. First-class evidence on the timeline, not a sidebar.

AI as a first-class citizen

  • Provider-agnostic by design. Anthropic is wired end-to-end today. Other providers and local models are designed into the architecture and will expand over time. Every call passes through the same consent gate.
  • Conversations are saved. Ask threads are searchable, resumable, and pinnable to Events and Dossiers. Your conversation history becomes part of your longitudinal learning.
  • Every output is auditable. A ModelRun record captures provider, prompt version, inputs sent, consent mode, and your final action. "Why did OwnChart say this?" always has an answer.
