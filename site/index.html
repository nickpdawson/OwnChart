Closer to Cursor for health and life data than to MyChart for patients.

OwnChart is not a record viewer. It is a research partner. You bring your data — every PDF, FHIR bundle, CCDA, HealthKit metric, faxed report, and personal note you can pull together — and OwnChart brings structure, memory, retrieval, citations, and AI-assisted reasoning. You ask questions of your own health life. OwnChart answers with evidence, cites every source, and admits what it doesn't know.

You stay the final authority on what anything means.

The doctrine

Six non-negotiables. Read them as constraints on every feature decision, not aspirations.

The patient owns it

Self-hosted on your hardware. No SaaS backend. No vendor telemetry. No institutional override. The patient is the user, the owner, and the canonical authority on their own story.

Raw sources are immutable

Original PDFs, FHIR bundles, CCDA XML are stored content-addressed by SHA-256 and never modified. The source is the record. Everything else is a derivation.

User correction is canonical

When the source says one thing and you say another, your version becomes the displayed truth. The source is preserved as evidence, never erased.

One consent gate, on egress

No PHI leaves the host to any LLM provider — Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, local — without your explicit, scoped opt-in. The gate is uniform across providers.

AI cites everything

Every substantive AI statement is source-backed, user-canonical, inferred, statistical, or explicitly unknown. Every claim opens to "why do you think that?"

Significance over fact-count

Source density is not meaning. A dense year of 1,200 extracted facts is not automatically a meaningful year. The product ranks by user-confirmable significance.

AI as a first-class citizen

  • Multi-provider. Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and local models (Ollama, llama.cpp). Pick a default; override per task. The consent gate is the same for all.
  • Conversations are saved. Ask and Make Sense threads are searchable, resumable, and pinnable to dossiers. Your conversation history becomes part of your longitudinal learning.
  • Every output is auditable. A ModelRun record captures provider, prompt version, inputs sent, consent mode, and your final action. "Why did OwnChart say this?" always has an answer.
